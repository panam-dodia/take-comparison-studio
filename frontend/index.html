Take Comparison Studio

Generate once, compare across 3 video models, keep full provenance.

Describe a subject once. This app generates a single reference image, then fans it out to 3 different video models (Kling, Pixverse, Veo), each conditioned on that same image. Compare the takes side by side, pick a favorite, and every asset's provenance is tracked back to that one shared reference.
